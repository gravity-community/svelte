# Energy

Two props ask for attention: `intent` says what kind (the colour), `energy` says how much. Never express how much attention through `intent`. An element sits on an energy level, and the wells around it can lower it but never raise it.

## Energy levels

Four levels, numbered from `3`, solid, the most excited, down to `0`, ghost. Any component with an `intent` draws its levels this way.

- Accent's hover steps toward mid grey, since it can't get deeper.
- A held toggle also changes its glyph, label or weight, not only its fill.
- Solid shows only at depth 0, on the page or an overlay: any filled well decays it, a grey page included, unless a `+n` shift lifts it back.
- Every solid fill clears 4.5:1 against its cut-out.
- Every level keeps the component's size; a level with no edge draws none, or a transparent one where the component has a border at another level.

- InputOtp at `1` draws framed tiles, one frame per character.
- A Checkbox is a field box: the soft fill and the faint frame, with the global focus outline and no invalid look yet. Checked, it lifts one level, as Choice's radio does: a `primary-solid` box with an `on-primary` check at `2`, the `primary-deep` tint inside a `border-focus` frame with a `primary-strong` check at `1`. Disabled, it is the plate at both levels: fainter than the soft box on the page, and a glaze that stays visible in a well.
- RadioGroup's radio is the same control on a disc, with a 6px dot. Invalid is the danger fill at `2` and the `danger-solid` ring at `1`, and the chosen radio while invalid lifts in danger: a `danger-solid` disc at `2`, `danger-deep` in a `danger-solid` ring at `1`.
- Intent cards (OptionCard and InfoCard with an `intent`) are soft in the intent's `-subtle` fill, hovering to `-subtle-hover`, and faint on the plate.

- Choice selects like a field at soft: its states change only the fill (checked `primary-subtle`, invalid and refused `danger-subtle`) and its radio carries the choice. At faint, checked is the selected mark, invalid the `danger-solid` edge, and refused the 2px `danger-solid` edge over `danger-subtle`.

## Potential and ground

Each component defines its range. Its potential is the highest level it takes: where it sits on the page, and its default. Its ground is the lowest level it takes. Left alone on the page, a component rests at its potential, and no well can take it below its ground.

- A text action in or just under a line of text, with no padding (a hint's "Enter manually", "Try again", "Cancel" under a form), is a `Link`, not a ghost Button: a Button always keeps its box and padding.

## Setting energy

`energy` takes an absolute level or a shift from the component's potential. Leave it out and the element sits at its default: its potential (ListItem: `0`), or inside a composed component the level of the slot it sits in.

    </tr>

- The blank page has the highest potential: at depth 0 a component reaches its potential, and each filled well lowers it by one level (see Decay).
- A shift decays with its well like any other level and stops at the component's range, so `+1` changes nothing on the page itself.
- Ghost reads best as `"0"`; a shift reads best for anything between the potential and the ground.
- Components don't know their well yet (callers decay by hand), so on the prop a `+n` stops at the potential, the same as leaving `energy` out. Until the planned Card passes depth down, lift an element with `resolveEnergy("+1", range, depth)`.
- Figma takes absolute levels only. To lift an instance the way `+1` does, give it a Depth mode one level shallower than its well.

## Decay

In a well of depth N, every element sits N levels below the level it was given, default, absolute or shifted, and stops at its ground. The deeper the well, the less energy an element keeps.

## Wells glaze their contents

Inside a filled well, a soft fill becomes a glaze of its own ink instead of the opaque `-subtle` fill, and the same glaze replaces the hover and held fills of faint and ghost. An opaque tint often all but vanishes on a grey or coloured well (1.00–1.14:1) and is gone on a well of the same intent; a glaze always sits a step off the well.

## Parts

A composed component is a well whose parts match the level it shows. Its definition gives a part a level of its own only where the part would steal attention from the whole; the well around the component lowers all of it together.

- In code, a part given no `energy` takes its slot's level, a shift still counts from the potential, and an absolute level ignores it. Don't decay a part by hand inside a composed component: it already follows its container.
- Soft on soft: a soft part on a fill of its own intent uses the deep glaze, 20% of the intent's solid colour (`neutral-solid` for neutral; a soft Button 15%, hovering to 20%). A part of another intent keeps the well's 10% glaze.
- A part never gets louder at a lower level. At faint and ghost, an IconCircle's or chip's glyph, and Callout's and Alert's icon, step to quieter shades of the intent (`<intent>-glyph-faint`, `<intent>-glyph-ghost`), one per intent and level, that never fall under 3:1 and, wherever 3:1 allows, stay at or under the level above. Labels keep the intent's `-strong` ink.
- Neutral has no primary decoration: a neutral card has a neutral chip, and primary appears only for state (checked, selected, active, current, done).
